Privacy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What stays on your device

Your points, gems, materials, settings and the maps you make are saved only on your own device. There is no account and no sign-up.

Bug reports

If you tap Send report, the game sends the game version, your device type, screen size, recent game log lines and anything you typed. It includes an anonymous device code (a scrambled ID), not your name, phone number or contacts. Reports are used only to fix the game.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own secure page. Lab Tanks never sees or stores your card details. We keep only the order code and which pack was bought, so the items can be delivered to your game once.

Battles with friends

Offline battles connect directly between the two devices on your Wi-Fi or hotspot. Nothing goes through our servers.

No ads, no tracking

The game has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools.
