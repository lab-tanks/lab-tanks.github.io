Terms & refunds

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The game

Lab Tanks is free to play. In-game packs are optional digital items (gems, points, building materials, refills, badges and paint jobs) used only inside the game. They have no cash value and can't be exchanged or transferred.

Delivery

Packs are added to your game right after payment. Every purchase also has a redeem code on your receipt: if the game was closed or lost signal, open the Store and tap Redeem. Each code can be redeemed once.

Refunds

If a pack never reached your game, contact us with your redeem code and we'll deliver it or refund you. Accidental purchases can be refunded within 14 days if the code has not been redeemed. Packs that were already delivered to a game can't be refunded.

Fair play

Don't cheat in battles with other players or try to break the purchase system. We may refuse service for abuse.
