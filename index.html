How it plays

Your tank, a cockpit full of gauges, a sweeping radar, and an island to hold.

🛡️

Drive & fight

Thumbstick on phones, WASD on computers. Knock out bunkers and enemy bots, earn points and gems.

🧱

Build mode

Walls, gates, roads, bridges over water, sand to make new land, and turrets. Pull walls back when you need them.

📡

Cockpit radar

Enemies show up only inside radar range, with a beep the moment one rolls in.

🏰

Defend your HQ

On HQ maps, the bots go for your headquarters when you wander off. Ten hits and it's over.

🗺️

Map Maker

Tap a square, pick what to build. Make your own islands from small to huge and play them right away.

🤝

Battle a friend, offline

Host or join over the same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. No internet, no servers, three lives each.
